S3. The match rule against history
----------------------------------
Input: every alert with state=fixed in the four repositories (63 + 68 + 13 + 45),
and every merged Dependabot PR, with its changed files (`gh pr view N --json files`).

Window: merged PRs whose mergedAt falls within 48 h before the alert's fixed_at.

Rule, as implemented in `match.jq` (scratchpad; task 03 writes the same program):
1. Updates: the body's `Updates \`X\` from A to B` rows if there are any; otherwise
   the title's `(bump|update) X [requirement] from A to B`.
2. Target version: B as written, or for a requirement, the `>=` or `==` version
   in it (`>=3.3.4,<4.0.0` gives 3.3.4). Anything else is no match.
3. Ecosystem: the branch slug mapped as above. Directory: the title or body
   directory, verified against the branch.
4. Package: compared case-insensitively. Scope name `pkg[extra]` gives `pkg`.
5. Match: same package, same ecosystem, same directory as `dirname(manifest)`,
   and the target version is at or above first_patched (the comparator tested
   on the 8 fixture cases in task 03).

Two corrections to C3 as drafted, both found by this run:
- Case: `PyJWT` in the alert and `pyjwt` in the PR. Without a case-insensitive compare
  the match count was 110 of 189; with it, 132 of 189. Fixed.
- Requirement form: `from >=0.53.0 to >=0.54.0` and `from <4.0.0,>=3.1.13 to >=3.3.4,<4.0.0`.
  Fixed by taking the lower bound.
- Body capture: `[^ ]+` ran past a line break into the release notes, so
  the body regex uses `[^[:space:]]+`. Fixed.
