# ralph vs backlog: review of 2026-11-02 (the result review)

**Window:** 2026-10-22 to 2026-11-02 (dispatch dates, UTC), read at about 18:30Z on
2026-11-02. **Runs:** `backlog` 124 (16 of them before the switch at 2026-10-22T21:28:06Z),
`ralph` 109. **Verdict: `ralph` wins (O24).** Both arms are past O3's 50 runs. `ralph` merges
more PRs per run, uses about half the box-hours for each merge, and costs less. The result is in
`result-2026-11-02.txt`, and ADR 0029 records the decision.

The second review by `13-reviewing-the-arms.md`, and the last one (task 12, step 9). The
numbers come from `ops/fabro-trial-review.sh` over the whole window. The overnight window
(2026-11-01 to 2026-11-02) was read separately, with `--traces`.

## Summary

- **O3's primary metric: `ralph` 0.35 merges per run against `backlog`'s 0.22** (38 of 109
  against 27 of 124), all with no operator commit. Two-proportion z ≈ 2.2 (p ≈ 0.03).
- **The guardrail holds by a wide margin:** 6.96 box-hours per merge against 13.77, which is
  −49% where the limit is +20%. Estimated hosted spend for both arms is about $66 a month,
  under the $100 limit, and `ralph` is $0.062 a run against `backlog`'s $0.154.
- **The win is not uniform.** It comes from flakeradar and womens-fantasy-sports.
  lawncare-saas favours `backlog` (8 of 37 against 4 of 30), mostly on coders-b. writers-app
  is a tie (6 of 30 against 6 of 26).
- **`ralph`'s largest hold is the `unverifiable` criterion, not the round budget.** In 31 of
  its 53 held PRs, the Refuter's last verdict failed only on `unverifiable` criteria, which never
  become Tasks (O8), so `merge_gate` refuses the merge. 15 PRs ended "The review did not end in
  a mergeable state", but in 9 of them the Refuter was holding too, so fixing the review would
  not have merged them. 5 had a Refuter that passed: 3 where the review's `error` came after the
  third Refute visit, and 2 where a reviewer asked for a human (`needs_human_review`).
  (Corrected on 2026-11-02: an earlier draft blamed the round budget for 12 of the 15.)
  `review_gate`'s `not_fixed` reason says "no Iteration round was left" on a Refuter hold too,
  which is what misled the first reading.
- **Decisions for the operator:** the follow-up series for O24's "ralph becomes the standard
  lane, `backlog` keeps the fast lane" (*Next*). `SCHEDULER_RALPH_SHARE` stays 50 until that
  series says otherwise.
